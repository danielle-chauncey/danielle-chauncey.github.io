## What It Is

A four-page joint solution brief for Commvault on AWS. It covers four offerings: Clumio, Cloud Rewind, Cleanroom Recovery, and Air Gap Protect. The layout follows Commvault's public design tokens, so it reads like a page from their own library.

## The Choices Behind It

**One audience.** Cloud-native teams with large and growing Amazon S3 estates: data lakes, analytics, and AI training data.

**The real alternative.** The native backup setup the team already runs. The brief doesn't knock it. It names the three moments a team outgrows it: the bill climbs, restores take hours, and backups share the blast radius.

**One recovery path.** Four product names become four steps, in the order a recovery runs: get the data back, rebuild the application, prove it is clean, keep a copy outside the blast radius. Each step goes from what the technology does, to the outcome, to the business value.

**A source for every claim.** Each number is a published customer result. Claims without a source stayed out.
