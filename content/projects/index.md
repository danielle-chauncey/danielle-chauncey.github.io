## Launch System

An opinionated framework for running product launches at AI and developer tools companies. Tier classification, messaging docs, competitive battlecards, launch briefs with kill criteria, and post-launch reviews that feed forward.

Built from experience launching developer platforms to 2M+ monthly users.

**What's in the repo:**
- Tier classification system (T1/T2/T3) with process requirements per tier
- 9 templates: messaging doc, launch brief, competitive battlecard, sales enablement one-pager, readiness checklist, content strategy brief, post-launch review, internal comms brief, developer announcement
- 5 guides: AI-native launches, developer audiences, PLG vs. sales-led, executive communications, metrics that matter
- Audience-signal mapping framework (matching proof types to personas)
- Launch philosophy doc (the "molecule model" for positioning, timing, channel, proof)

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/danielle-chauncey/launch-system)

## AI-Native Marketing Workflows

Agent-based systems I built and operate daily. MCP-powered document assembly, RAG knowledge bases, and LLM editorial governance.

**What I built:**
- **MCP workflow orchestration** for executive briefing materials. Pulls data from 8+ teams, assembles structured documents, runs quality checks. 70% of program operations now run through these systems.
- **RAG knowledge base** indexing 14,000+ files for conversational retrieval. Replaced manual search with natural language queries against the full document corpus.
- **LLM editorial review** trained on a style guide to catch tone, terminology, and structural violations before human review. Integrated into CI/CD publishing.

Stack: Claude, MCP, Python, Git, vector storage

*Public repo in progress.*
