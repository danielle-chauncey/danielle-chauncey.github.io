The challenge

Native backup works early. Then your data outgrows it.

Most cloud-native teams start with what is already in the account: versioning, replication, snapshots, and native backups. That is a good start. The fit changes as S3 turns into the home for data lakes, analytics, and AI training data.

TRIGGER 01

The bill climbs

Backup cost grows every year with the data, even when nothing else changes.

TRIGGER 02

Restores take hours

Large recoveries wait on a full rehydration before anyone can use the data.

TRIGGER 03

Backups share the blast radius

Copies that live in the production account are exposed when that account is compromised.

"In the beginning, AWS-native backups worked well for us. ... Over time, as our data grew, our AWS backup bill grew with it."

Lida Joly, CEO, xyzt.ai, in Commvault's customer story (January 2026)

How it works

One recovery path, in the order a recovery runs.

Customers ask what happens, step by step, when something goes wrong. Each step below runs from what the technology does, to what changes for the team, to why the business cares.

Step
Outcome
Business value
1

Get the data back

Clumio by Commvault

Immutable, air-gapped backups for S3, DynamoDB, RDS, Aurora, EC2, and EBS. Instant Access mounts a backup before a full restore finishes.

Outcome

Teams work with recovered data in minutes, then complete the full restore.

Value

Less downtime on the data your product and your AI work depend on.

2

Rebuild the application

Commvault Cloud Rewind

Captures point-in-time, in-sync copies of the compute, networking, and configuration around the data. No hand-written infrastructure code.

Outcome

The whole application comes back: data, compute, networking, and configuration.

Value

Recovery measured in minutes against days or weeks of manual rebuild.

3

Prove it is clean

Commvault Cleanroom Recovery

Provisions an isolated recovery location in AWS for restore, forensics, and regular recovery tests.

Outcome

Security and IT confirm a clean copy before it returns to production.

Value

Lower reinfection risk, and a recovery plan that has been rehearsed.

4

Keep a copy outside the blast radius

Commvault Air Gap Protect

Immutable, isolated copies held as a service in a Commvault tenant, separate from customer accounts.

Outcome

A trusted recovery point survives an account compromise.

Value

A last line of defense that auditors and boards can see.
